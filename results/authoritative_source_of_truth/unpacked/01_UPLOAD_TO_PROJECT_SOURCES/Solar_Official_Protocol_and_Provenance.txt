KEY PROTOCOL INVARIANTS
- Common direct/LOCO test evaluation uses the adopted 2024-2025 test period and a common 168 h eligibility warm-up.
- H24 direct test residuals: 6,186,600; daylight cases: 3,103,207.
- H48 direct test residuals: 12,355,920; daylight cases: 6,199,242.
- Repeated neural systems retain seeds 1, 2, and 3 and report sample SD with ddof=1.
- Direct LSTM uses the multivariate leakage-controlled predictor representation; official PatchTST uses S-mode with strictly past normalized PV alone. LSTM-vs-PatchTST comparisons are therefore end-to-end forecasting-system comparisons, not pure same-input architecture ablations.
- PatchTST official repository commit: 204c21efe0b39603ad6e2ca640ef5896646ab1a9.
- Direct PatchTST pre-test selection locks confirm selection_used_test=false and L168 for all retained global/location-specific H24/H48 configurations.
- LOCO cold-start excludes target-derived PV-history predictors. LOCO history-aware may use strictly past target-site PV history at inference while excluding the target site from source fitting.
- Few-shot adaptation is head-only; the recurrent backbone, source scalers, and source validation correction remain frozen.
- Statistical support requires BOTH a confidence interval excluding zero AND Holm-adjusted p < 0.05. Direct effect sign is PatchTST - LSTM; positive values favor LSTM.
